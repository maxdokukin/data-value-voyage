Gini Coefficient Analysis About US Presidents, Wartime Events, and Major Economic Recessions.

The Gini Coefficient is a measure of income inequality within a population, but it is also a lagging indicator, meaning it reflects past events rather than current conditions.

  • The US Presidents don't seem to have a significant impact on the Gini Coefficient, there is no notable trend between Republic (red blocks) and Democrat (blue blocks) presidents.
  • The Wartime events seem to have a more obvious impact on the Gini Coefficient, especially around WWII, The Korean War and the Vietnam War.
  • Major Economic Recessions don't seem to have a significant impact on the Gini Coefficnent.
